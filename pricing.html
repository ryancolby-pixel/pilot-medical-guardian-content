Free to use: your renewal countdown and reminders, unlimited record-keeping (application details, medications, Item 18 answers, doctor visits), the medication reference, Health, and the AME directory. Your first MedXPress autofill is free for 7 days from your first Fill. After that, autofill every renewal is part of Guardian Pro. An optional Guardian Pro subscription unlocks MedXPress autofill every cycle, the clean CMEC export, Share with your AME, My Documents, Special Issuance tracking, the CACI worksheet in the app, and the prior-cycle answer comparison. $29.99 a year, auto-renews until canceled.

Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple account settings. One subscription covers all your devices, so if you subscribe on your iPhone, Guardian Pro is already active on your iPad and your Mac.

The extension is free to install and works with your Guardian Pro subscription. Your free first autofill is on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, in the app or in Safari.
