Methylcellulose (Citrucel) and the FAA medical

Citrucel · Fiber laxative

FAA document updated Oct 27, 2026Checked Nov 3, 2026FAA source

Allowed in the FAA MedGuide. No wait listed after a dose.

Brands: Citrucel
Underlying condition: Constipation
Frequency limit: Per label directions
Initial and subsequent wait: None
Discontinuation wait (after final dose): None

The FAA defines allowed as "acceptable for use while performing flight or safety-related duties, provided the condition is stable/aeromedically acceptable and no adverse side effects are present."

MedGuide says "medications with minimal systemic effects may be shortened."

MedGuide adds that "an extended observation period may be required to verify clinical stability of the condition" and that it is not "the sole basis in making aeromedical determinations".

Source: FAA MedGuide, version 1.0, updated October 27, 2026.

That is what the FAA publishes, not a determination about you.

Source

  • FAA MedGuide, Guide for Aviation Medical Examiners (version 1.0, updated 10-27-2026)
