MedXPress autofill on a Windows PC

Keep your records in Pilot Medical Guardian on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. On the PC, a Chrome or Edge extension fills MedXPress from those records. There's no Windows app.

Needs Guardian Pro, which you get in the app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

See the setup steps

Your records stay in your own iCloud. The extension keeps its copy only in the browser's memory, and sends nothing to me.
