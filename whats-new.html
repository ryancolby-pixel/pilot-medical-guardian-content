What's new, and what's next

Pilot Medical Guardian is built in the open, alongside the pilots who use it. Here's what's recently landed in the app and what I'm working on now. The app is actively developed, so things move quickly. This page is a snapshot, not a schedule.

Recently shipped

Now in the app

Most of this is free: your renewal countdown and reminders, unlimited record-keeping (application details, medications, Item 18 answers, doctor visits), the medication reference, Health, and the AME directory. Your first MedXPress autofill is free for 7 days from your first Fill. After that, autofill every renewal is part of Guardian Pro. The items marked Guardian Pro are part of the optional subscription.

  • MedXPress autofill fills your whole form: Guardian Pro Items 1 to 16, medications, Item 18 (every No, and up to 15 Yes answers with your explanations) and doctor visits. Save your application details once (address, certificates, employer, flight time). After you click Add on a medication or visit, the next one fills itself. MedXPress fills your name and Social Security number itself.
  • See what autofill filled: filled fields turn yellow, MedXPress's Add button is outlined, and a card at the top counts where you are. Fixed: a visit or medication you filled but did not add could be skipped.
  • MedXPress autofill on a Windows PC: Guardian Pro an extension for Chrome and Edge fills Items 1 to 19 from the records you keep in the app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. There's no Windows app. Sign in with your Apple Account, or open an encrypted file from Send to a computer on your iPhone or iPad. MedXPress Prep has a Want to autofill on a PC? button with the steps, or see the setup steps.
  • MedXPress Prep, in form order: on iPhone and iPad, Prep is now one list in the same order as the MedXPress form: Items 1 to 16, medications, Item 18, then doctor visits. Autofill and More ways to fill sit pinned at the bottom. Prep and Home also tell you when your Items 1 to 16 details are missing.
  • Picks up where you left off: the app reopens on the same tab and screen after iOS closes it in the background.
  • Reminders with notifications off: Home and Special Issuance offer Add to my calendar, and your reminders come back once you allow notifications. On iPhone and iPad.
  • Class fix: some Class 2 and Class 3 pilots saw no expiration date because Class you need was stuck on Class 1. The app matches it to your certificate once and shows a note saying what changed.
  • App lock: with the lock on, window titles no longer show what you were viewing.
  • Guardian Pro, explained up front: the intro has a page on what Guardian Pro adds, with the price.
  • Easier to read: clearer wording and a better fit at larger text sizes across the app.
  • Email your AME, already addressed: Guardian Pro start Share with my AME from an AME in the directory, or from the AME you marked as yours, and the app fills in the email the FAA's directory lists for them. Check it's where the office wants records sent. One button then opens Apple Mail with your PDF attached and the address, subject and a short note filled in. No Apple Mail account? It copies the address and opens the share sheet for your email app. Email isn't end-to-end encrypted, so the PDF stays in your Sent mail and your AME's inbox until someone deletes it. On iPhone and iPad.
  • Fits MedXPress better: pick a medication's frequency from MedXPress's own 13 choices, so the autofill can fill it. Older entries are matched when the meaning is the same; if one has no match, the app asks you to pick. If a saved address has a symbol MedXPress rejects, like # or a dash, the app points it out and leaves the fix to you. On iPhone and iPad.
  • Autofill setup, easier to find: on iPhone and iPad, a Turn on MedXPress autofill card in My Records, and the button pinned at the bottom of MedXPress Prep, open the setup steps. Once it's set up, the card shrinks to a small row you can always come back to for help.
  • Fewer blanks on MedXPress: no medications or no doctor visits? Answer Item 17a and Item 19 once in Application Details and the autofill fills No. It also stops at MedXPress's 20-visit limit.
  • My Records in MedXPress order: Application Details, Medications, Item 18, then Doctor Visits, the order you fill out the form.
  • Deferral letters: if the FAA defers your medical, track the letter in Special Issuance: the date, the response time it states, what it asks for and what you've sent.
  • Several visit dates at once: add a repeat doctor visit, like physical therapy, with all its dates in one go.
  • Every FAA AME, with addresses: the AME directory now lists every AME on the FAA's Designee Locator, with each office's street address and the HIMS-trained ones marked. Tap an address to open it in Maps. I refresh it about once a month. Free.
  • Share with my AME, sharper: Guardian Pro the PDF is titled for your AME and fills in their name from the AME you marked as yours. Your Item 18 Yes answers and explanations go in unless you switch them off.
  • Special Issuance due dates: Guardian Pro give each requirement a due date and see them on a timeline, overdue first.
  • FAA disposition tables: answer Yes on an Item 18 question, or search a condition like GERD, and the app lists the FAA's own disposition tables for it, each with its date, and opens the FAA's PDF right in the app. Free.
  • Search by type: search "statin", "muscle relaxer" or "BP med" and see every medication of that kind, grouped under the FAA status line each one carries. Free.
  • Did you mean: misspell a medication when you add it and the app suggests the name you probably meant. Nothing is linked until you tap it. Free.
  • FAA MedGuide wait times: the medication reference now includes the FAA's new MedGuide (October 2026). For the drugs it lists you see the wait after your first dose, after each dose, and after you stop. If two FAA pages give different numbers, the app shows both with their dates. It doesn't pick one. Free.
  • A bigger medication reference: almost 600 medications now. Each one shows what the FAA page that names it says, with the source, or tells you plainly when the FAA doesn't name it. That covers the Do Not Issue and Do Not Fly lists, the CACI worksheets, the FAA's biologics list and the OTC chart. Free.
  • FAA reference search: ask a plain question, like how long a first class medical lasts or whether a medication is a problem, and get the FAA's own answer back with the citation, the date that entry was last verified, and a link to the FAA's page. It covers medications, thresholds, certificate durations, BasicMed, CACI, Special Issuance requirements, the Item 18 questions and the FAA's disposition tables. Searching runs on your device and never touches your records. Free.
  • A Mac app: MedXPress is a desktop form, and now you can autofill it on the same machine you fill it out on. The same records and the same paperwork prep, on a full keyboard and a big screen. It syncs through your own iCloud exactly the way your iPhone and iPad already do, so nothing new leaves your devices.
  • BasicMed (Part 68) support: a plain-language guide to the FAA's BasicMed requirements linked straight to the source, plus tracking for your medical-education course (every 24 months) and physician exam (every 48 months) with reminders before each is due. Tell the app how you fly (certificate, BasicMed, or both) and it tracks the right dates for your path.
  • BasicMed CMEC form prep: fills the pilot section of the FAA's Comprehensive Medical Examination Checklist (Form 8700-2) from the records you've already entered. Filling and previewing it is free. Guardian Pro exports a clean copy without the watermark, ready to hand your physician. You review and sign it yourself.
  • Share with my AME: Guardian Pro bundle your records and saved documents into one clean PDF and send it to your AME from your phone; you choose what to include, and it's built on your device.
  • Mail it to the FAA: Guardian Pro when you need to send documents to the FAA by mail, the app assembles a print-ready package: a cover sheet plus the documents you choose, with your name and date of birth on every page the way the FAA asks, so it's ready for the envelope.
  • Bring in your documents: Guardian Pro add a PDF or photo from Files or Photos, or share one straight into the app from Mail (like a lab result your doctor emailed), alongside the camera scanner, and keep them organized in one place.
  • Faster data entry: log a visit from Siri, Shortcuts, or the Action button; pull a provider's details from your Contacts; your most recent providers at the top of the list when you pick one for a visit; one-tap chips for recent reasons; and add another visit from the same provider in a tap.
  • Provider suggestions: when you log a visit, the app suggests the clinic names and addresses you've already saved, as you type. They come only from your own records, on your device.
  • Calendar import for your visits: if you keep doctor's appointments on one calendar, connect it and the app offers each appointment as a visit for you to confirm. It reads only the calendar you connect. On iPhone and iPad.
  • Apple Health Records import: pull your real medications and lab results from your healthcare provider, on your device, to speed up your prep.
  • Home & Lock Screen widget: a glanceable countdown to your medical's expiration, right on your Home or Lock Screen.
  • Siri & Spotlight: ask "when does my medical expire?" out loud, or jump into the app straight from search.
  • Calendar reminders: add your renewal deadline to your own calendar so it reminds you however you've set it up.
  • Save your MedXPress confirmation number: when you submit, one tap on the FAA's own confirmation page saves the number into the app, with the date you submitted and the date it expires. That page does not come back once you leave it, and your AME cannot open your application without the number. It shows on your Home screen ready to copy at the exam. Saving the number is free. It also prints on your Share with my AME packet, which is Guardian Pro.
  • MedXPress autofill: Guardian Pro fills your records straight into the FAA form so you don't retype them every cycle.
  • CACI worksheets: Guardian Pro when one of your Item 18 Yes answers matches a condition the FAA covers with a CACI Worksheet, the FAA's worksheet opens right in the app, offline, to read, share, or print. Without Guardian Pro you still get the FAA citation and a link to it on faa.gov.
  • Prior-cycle answer check: Guardian Pro snapshots each cycle's prep and flags when this cycle's answers drift from last time.
  • Special Issuance tracking: Guardian Pro keeps your SI requirements, checklist, and physician letters organized, with reminders for recurring tests.
  • Health metrics vs. the FAA's own thresholds: charts your Apple Health data against the FAA's cited numbers, with the source shown.
  • Renewal countdown: calculates your certificate's expiration from the real 14 CFR rules and reminds you before it lapses.
  • AME directory: searchable, factual list of Aviation Medical Examiners, sortable by distance and plotted on a map.
  • iPad layout: a proper sidebar on iPad, plus polish across the app.
  • iPhone Duo: the app fills the whole inner screen, and when the Duo is folded like a book, the sidebar sits on one side of the fold and the screen you're reading on the other.
