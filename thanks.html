What happens next

  1. Get it from the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Free to download.
  2. Install it, open it, and follow the short setup: add your certificate or your BasicMed dates, then your medications and doctor visits.
  3. Turn on the MedXPress autofill when you're ready: the app walks you through enabling the Safari extension.

Worth a look

Have a look at the FAQ. It covers what the app does, how your data stays private, how it's different from the services you may already use, and how to get started. When you're ready to set up the MedXPress autofill, the step-by-step setup guide walks you through it. Anything else, just email support@pilotmedicalguardian.com and it reaches me directly.
