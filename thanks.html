Now on the App Store

It's live

Pilot Medical Guardian is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Thanks for signing up and for waiting. It's just me building this. Your email is separate from the app and never touches your medical data.

Download on the App Store →

What happens next

  1. Get it from the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Free to download.
  2. Install it, open it, and follow the short setup: add your certificate or your BasicMed dates, then your application details, medications, Item 18 answers and doctor visits.
  3. Fill MedXPress when you're ready: the FAA's site opens inside the app, with no extension to set up. Prefer Safari? The app walks you through turning on the extension.

Worth a look

Have a look at the FAQ. It covers what the app does, how your data stays private, how it's different from the services you may already use, and how to get started. For the MedXPress autofill, in the app or in Safari, the step-by-step guide walks you through it. Anything else, just email support@pilotmedicalguardian.com and it reaches me directly.
