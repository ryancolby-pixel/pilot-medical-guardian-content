What it does

Three jobs, before you sit down at MedXPress

Autofill MedXPress

Autofill is now one tap: Fill MedXPress opens the FAA's own MedXPress site right inside the app, with no Safari setup. Fill on each page puts your application details (Items 1 to 16), medications (Item 17), every Item 18 answer with your explanations, and doctor visits (Item 19) into the FAA form, so you are not retyping every renewal. You review every field and submit it yourself. Prefer Safari? The extension still works.
