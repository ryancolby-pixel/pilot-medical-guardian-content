The FAA's online application

What FAA MedXPress is, and how to get through it

MedXPress is the FAA's free web application for submitting Form 8500-8 before your exam, at medxpress.faa.gov. You create an account, complete Items 1 through 20, submit, and bring the confirmation number to your AME. It is the application only. You still need the exam.

Two deadlines that catch pilots out. An application created but not submitted is removed after 30 days. Once submitted, your exam must happen within 60 days or the application is deleted and you start again. Full walkthrough of Items 17, 18 and 19.

This app is an information and record-keeping tool. It is not medical, legal, or FAA compliance certification, and it is not medical advice. It helps you stay organized; your AME makes the certification decisions.
